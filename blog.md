# Introduction

## About Me

I am a college student developing my skills in data science, programming, and data analysis. Through my coursework, I have been learning how to use Python to work with real-world datasets, clean and organize data, analyze trends, and communicate findings through visualizations.

This portfolio is a collection of my academic work and projects as I continue building my technical and professional skills. One of the first projects included in my portfolio focuses on electric vehicles and charging infrastructure in the United States. For this project, I worked with data from the U.S. Department of Energy's Alternative Fuels Data Center to examine whether charging infrastructure is growing fast enough to support the growth of electric vehicles. I used Python, pandas, Seaborn, and Matplotlib to collect, clean, combine, and analyze the data.

Working on this project helped me understand that data analysis is more than creating charts or calculating numbers. It is also important to ask a clear research question, understand where the data comes from, recognize limitations, and explain what the results actually mean. I also gained more experience troubleshooting code and working through errors while developing my analysis.

As I continue my education, I want to strengthen my programming, analytical, and problem-solving skills and gain more experience working with real-world data. My goal is to build a portfolio that shows my growth over time and demonstrates my ability to use data to answer meaningful questions.

This portfolio will continue to grow as I complete more projects and develop new skills.
