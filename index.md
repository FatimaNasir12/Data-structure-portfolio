<h2>Featured Project</h2>

<div class="project-card">

<h3>🚗 Electric Vehicles & Charging Infrastructure</h3>

<p>
<strong>Research Question:</strong>
<br>
Is charging infrastructure growing fast enough to support electric vehicle growth?
</p>

<p>
I analyzed U.S. electric vehicle registrations and charging infrastructure
from <strong>2016–2025</strong> using data from the U.S. Department of Energy's
Alternative Fuels Data Center.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Tools:</strong>
Python · pandas · Seaborn · Matplotlib
</p>

<p>
<strong>Key Finding:</strong>
</p>

<p>
EV registrations increased from approximately <strong>280,300</strong>
in 2016 to more than <strong>5.6 million</strong> in 2025, while charging
outlets increased from <strong>44,299</strong> to <strong>271,488</strong>.
</p>

<p>
The analysis suggests that EV adoption has grown faster than charging
infrastructure.
</p>
