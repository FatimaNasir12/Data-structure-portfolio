## Featured Project

### Electric Vehicles and Charging Infrastructure

**Research Question:**  
Is charging infrastructure growing fast enough to support electric vehicle growth?

In this project, I use Python and real-world data from the U.S. Department of Energy's Alternative Fuels Data Center to compare electric vehicle registrations with charging infrastructure from 2016 to 2025.

I used:

- Python
- pandas
- Seaborn
- Matplotlib
- Data analysis and visualization
