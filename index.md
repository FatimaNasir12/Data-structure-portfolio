### 🚗 Electric Vehicles & Charging Infrastructure

**Research Question:**  
*Is charging infrastructure growing fast enough to support electric vehicle growth?*

I analyzed U.S. electric vehicle registrations and charging infrastructure from **2016–2025** using data from the U.S. Department of Energy's Alternative Fuels Data Center.

**Tools:** Python · pandas · Seaborn · Matplotlib

### Key Finding

EV registrations increased from approximately **280,300** in 2016 to more than **5.6 million** in 2025, while charging outlets increased from **44,299** to **271,488**.

The analysis suggests that EV adoption has grown faster than charging infrastructure.
